# Precision corpus

Pinned open-source projects scanned nightly (`corpus/projects.json`), with canonical findings
committed under `corpus/expected/` and verdicts under `corpus/adjudications/`. Every analysis
change re-scans the corpus and adjudicates each changed row here before `corpus/accept.sh` records
it; the checklist is in [VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md#precision-corpus).

A project's sample is every expected finding whose key (path, line, column, kind, name) hashes
under the project's `sample_rate` in `corpus/adjudications/<project>.json`, a rate fixed at about 30
findings per project when the hash sample was introduced on 2026-09-29. A finding that survives an
analysis change keeps its membership and its verdict, a removed one drops out, and a new one may
enter and need a verdict, so each corpus diff asks for a few verdicts rather than a new sample.
`corpus/sample.py <project>` lists the sample; `corpus/precision.py` lists what needs a verdict.

When the hash sample was introduced, 13 of its 81 findings already had a verdict from the first
measurement below or from the enum-case change; the other 68 were adjudicated on 2026-09-29 (see
the second measurement below). The 146 earlier verdicts are recorded in `corpus/adjudications/` with their original IDs
(`Alamofire-1`, or `enum-case-1` for the enum-case table); 18 are on findings no longer reported
and are marked retired.
